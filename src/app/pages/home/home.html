Serbia on 21 October 2026

President Aleksandar Vučić resigned on 27 September to seek the prime minister’s post in snap parliamentary elections on 25 October. The vote follows nearly two years of student-led protests that began after the Novi Sad railway station collapse of November 2024. This site does not cover the result.

Sources and notes

What this site relies on, and what to check before you quote it.

How it was put together

Events from 2026, including the resignation of President Vučić, the 25 October election, the state of the Kosovo dialogue and the EU–Western Balkans summit, were checked against news and official sources on 21 October 2026. They are linked on each chapter.

Earlier history follows standard accounts, court records and the books listed below. Dates, figures and legal findings were written with care, but some of them, such as casualty numbers and refugee estimates, are disputed or revised over time. Where a figure is an estimate, the text says so.

Where historians and governments disagree, each chapter has a block called “Where accounts differ” that sets out the positions and who holds them. The site does not claim to settle these arguments. The unfinished trial of Slobodan Milošević and the unresolved political background of the Đinđić assassination are two cases where a court did not, or could not, deliver the final word.

The site is a snapshot. It does not report the result of the 25 October 2026 election.
